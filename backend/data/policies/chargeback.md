# Disputes and escalation
Reviewed: 2026-10-09. Jurisdiction: India.

## Verified regulator source
RBI Integrated Ombudsman Scheme 2026 FAQ: https://www.rbi.org.in/commonperson/english/scripts/FAQs.aspx?Id=3407
Approach the regulated entity first and retain acknowledgement. For no reply, the FAQ specifies 30 days or the applicable RBI/NPCI/card-network period, whichever is higher; dissatisfaction with a received reply is a separate route. It specifies filing within 90 days of the applicable period ending or the entity's last communication, whichever is later. Other eligibility conditions apply. Check the current scheme before submission: https://rbidocs.rbi.org.in/rdocs/content/pdfs/SCHEME16012026_A.pdf
Official filing portal: https://cms.rbi.org.in/

## Provider-specific source
Paytm's UPI terms describe complaint escalation from Paytm to PSP bank, account-holding bank, and NPCI: https://paytm.com/company/upi-terms-and-conditions (Dispute & Grievance). This verifies Paytm's published workflow, not every app's current procedure. The NPCI legacy dispute page could not be retrieved during review; consult the current official NPCI support route at https://upihelp.npci.org.in/.

## Product operating guidance
A chargeback is an issuer/network dispute process, not a guaranteed refund. Do not invent universal network filing deadlines. Capture evidence, original reference, merchant communications, and prior complaint dates. Ask the issuer for the applicable reason code and deadline. Do not submit a complaint on the customer's behalf without explicit authorization. Route suspected unauthorized payments urgently to the bank; do not ask the customer to wait for a normal pending-payment window.
