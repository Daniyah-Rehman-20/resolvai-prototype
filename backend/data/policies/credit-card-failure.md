# Credit card failures
Reviewed: 2026-10-09. Jurisdiction: India. Informational guidance; applicability must be confirmed from transaction evidence.

Source: RBI/2019-20/67, 20 September 2019. https://www.rbi.org.in/Commonperson/english/Scripts/Notification.aspx?Id=3074
Use the card incident classification in debit-card-failure.md; the circular classifies by payment incident, not a universal credit-card refund period.

## Product investigation guidance
Distinguish declined purchase, authorization hold, captured purchase, refund, and disputed or unauthorized charge. Ask the issuer to establish the ledger status and applicable dispute route. Do not advise withholding bill payment or promise interest/fee waiver. A merchant cancellation or return is not automatically a failed-payment reversal. Route billing and unauthorized-payment issues to the issuer promptly.
