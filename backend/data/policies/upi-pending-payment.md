# UPI pending and failed payments
Reviewed: 2026-10-09. Jurisdiction: India. Informational guidance; applicability must be confirmed from transaction evidence.

## Source-backed rules
Source: RBI/2019-20/67, 20 September 2019; effective 15 October 2019. https://www.rbi.org.in/Commonperson/english/Scripts/Notification.aspx?Id=3074
Domestic transactions only. T is the transaction calendar date, not a business day. For a debited UPI transfer with an uncredited beneficiary, reversal is due by T+1 if credit cannot be completed. For a debited merchant payment lacking merchant confirmation, reversal is due by T+5. Applicable delay compensation is INR 100 per day beyond the respective deadline and is payable without a customer claim.

## Product investigation guidance
First distinguish person-to-person from merchant payment. Collect reference, date, amount, app, and bank; confirm debit and beneficiary/merchant status. A generic pending label is insufficient to decide eligibility. Do not issue a second refund while final status is unknown. Show the relevant source and request human review for inconsistent evidence. These are regulatory outer limits, not a promise of recovery by this application. Merchant returns and successful payments sent to the wrong recipient require separate investigation.
