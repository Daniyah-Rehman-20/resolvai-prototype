# Merchant refunds
Reviewed: 2026-10-09. Type: product operating guidance, not a regulator-set refund SLA.

A merchant refund reverses a previously successful purchase under the merchant/provider process. Do not apply a failed-transaction deadline merely because the customer uses the word refund. Obtain the original payment reference, merchant refund confirmation, refund reference, initiation time, amount, and provider status. Distinguish requested, accepted, processing, failed, and credited. An application case or approval is not proof that money moved.

Use the specific merchant and provider contract for processing estimates; no universal 5-to-7-day or 7-to-10-day promise is established by this knowledge base. Missing provider evidence means manual review. A refund recommendation requires ownership checks, remaining refundable amount, duplicate-action prevention, and authorized approval before any live provider request. Customer consent and provider authorization are separate requirements. If no live adapter exists, label the action as a request, never a completed refund.
